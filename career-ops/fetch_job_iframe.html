Overview

Software development is changing fast. And we are building a new way of doing it.

At Nortal, we believe complexity does not have to mean complicated. As systems grow larger and expectations keep rising, the challenge isn't just writing code. It is building software that stays reliable, maintainable, and easy to evolve.

That's where this role comes in. From modern Java development to large-scale digital services, you'll tackle problems that require curiosity, collaboration, and thoughtful engineering.

Sound like the kind of engineering you enjoy? Then let's build it together!

What you’ll do

  • Work with cross-functional teams to build and maintain high-quality Java solutions
  • Participate in the full software development life cycle, including requirements gathering, design, coding, testing, and deployment
  • Apply architectural patterns and best practices to ensure high scalability and performance
  • Track industry advances in technologies and methodologies to help improve products and workflows

What makes you a great fit 

  • Proven track record in Java software development
  • Familiarity with modern front-end frameworks, SOA, and contemporary databases
  • Experience writing unit tests, refactoring, and maintaining existing codebases
  • Strong understanding of software design patterns and Agile methodologies

  • Curious, pragmatic, and open to rethinking existing solutions

  • Proficiency in Estonian and English

Why join Nortal?

  • Work at the forefront of AI-driven software development
  • Access to real-world projects where new development approaches can be tested at scale
  • Continuous learning and career growth with mentorship, knowledge sharing, and dedicated development opportunities 
  • The freedom to design your workday where and when you're most productive
  • A focus on health and wellbeing through extra health insurance and sports initiatives
  • Supportive and low-hierarchy culture surrounded by sharp, inspiring tech experts 

Salary range

  • For mid level position 2900-4500€ gross/monthly (full-time).
  • For senior level position 4600-6000€ gross/monthly (full-time).

Final compensation will depend on factors such as relevant experience, role seniority, and skill set.

 

About Nortal

For over 25 years, we’ve been shaping digital transformation, delivering complex solutions for global enterprises and public sector organisations. Today, we are at the forefront of an AI-driven transformation era, helping organizations turn data into real business value. By combining deep domain expertise with data and AI, we design solutions that create immediate impact while scaling for the future.
